TECHCORP CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ — ARCHIVED ANSWERS
Last reviewed: March 2024

Refund requests
Support agents often receive questions about the 14-day refund window. The current, binding policy is maintained in the Internal Policy & Engineering Handbook. This FAQ is not an authority for refund decisions.

Annual-plan discussions
Customers sometimes use the word “cancellation” when they mean a refund request. Annual-plan cancellation, partial refunds, and full refunds can have different eligibility rules. Support must verify the customer’s plan and purchase date before responding.

Incident communications
For a customer-reported outage, Support opens a ticket and shares the public status page. Support agents do not classify SEV levels, set PagerDuty response times, or notify executives.

Password help
Support cannot reset an employee password. Employee authentication requirements and account lockout rules are owned by IT Security.
